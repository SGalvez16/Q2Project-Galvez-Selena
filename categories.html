Recipe Categories

Adobo

A savory and delicious Filipino dish made with pork or chicken, marinated in vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, and spices.

Lumpia

A Filipino spring roll filled with vegetables and/or meat, often served with a sweet dipping sauce.

Sisig

A sizzling Filipino dish made from pork, seasoned with calamansi and served on a hot plate.

Pancit

A Filipino noodle dish, often cooked with vegetables, meat, and seafood, perfect for celebrations.

Sinigang

A sour soup often made with pork, shrimp, or fish, with vegetables like kangkong and eggplant.
